The Story

Tears for Clavel is a singleplayer narrative murder mystery game where you play the same story from two different perspectives. As a night watchman during the dark and as a young mailman during the day, with a murder that ties everything together towards the end. It's set in a fictional 1940s post civil war Spain, with handrawn stylized art.
